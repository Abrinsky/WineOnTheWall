About

Steel craftsmanship for wine display

Wine On the Wall began as a specialty line from Sioux City Foundry Co., translating Midwest steel manufacturing know-how into refined, wall-mounted wine storage.

Origin

A better way to show the collection

When I was trying to find a solution to displaying my own wine collection, I was not able to find a product that suited my needs. I decided to put my knowledge and experience in the steel manufacturing industry into creating a unique product and developed a whole new way to display and store wine. Andy Galinsky, CEO, Sioux City Foundry Co.
Made with intent

Midwest stainless, modern presentation

Racks are made of stainless steel and produced in the Midwest. The goal is a streamlined showcase for wine enthusiasts across price points, without the bulk of traditional wood systems.

This sample redesign emphasizes a high-class, contemporary stainless aesthetic: dark surfaces, brushed steel, and warm wine accents. It is not the live corporate site.
